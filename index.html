Regulated payments · B2B · Compliance

Scaling a regulated payments product

Verification rates for complex business customers were around 2%. Research showed the compliance framework didn't match how those customers are actually structured, and led leadership to rebuild it.

Result: verification success from ~2% to 23%, ~9,000 business customers onboarded.

How I work

Research that outlives the readout

Start with the gap, not the brief

I identify what the organisation doesn't yet know, widen narrow briefs before fieldwork, and make the case for the research that is needed.

Qual depth, then quant scale

Interviews surface mental models and trade-offs, which then shape the conjoint, MaxDiff and survey work that builds credible business cases.

Findings become knowledge

Frameworks, primers and measurement tools that teams keep using, rather than one-off slide decks. I also coach stakeholders to run their own studies.
