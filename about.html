About

Research at the intersection of users, product and regulation

Hello, I'm Alessandra

I'm a Senior UX Researcher with over eight years at Booking.com, where I've specialised in B2B payments, fintech and regulatory compliance. My work sits between user experience, product strategy and legal requirements, a space where surface-level research rarely cuts it.

I design and lead multi-stage research programs end-to-end: from identifying knowledge gaps and scoping with stakeholders, through fieldwork and analysis, to translating findings into formats that move decisions at VP and Director level. I work across qualitative and quantitative methods, including conjoint, MaxDiff, experience measurement and large-scale surveys.

I believe a researcher's job doesn't end at the readout. I work to make findings organisational knowledge, through frameworks, primers and embedding research thinking across functions, rather than one-off slide decks. I also coach Product and Design stakeholders to run their own studies and mentor junior researchers.
