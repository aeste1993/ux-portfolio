Shaping a 5-year B2B payments strategy through multi-stage research

Closing critical knowledge gaps across siloed teams to give a complex payments business a shared evidence base for long-term decisions.

The challenge

The B2B payments business needed a strategic foundation to guide product investment over a 5-year horizon, but critical knowledge gaps existed across siloed Commercial, Product and GTM teams. There was no shared understanding of customer financial needs, no way to quantify the value of competing product features, and no experience measurement framework to track the customer relationship over time.

I identified these gaps proactively, before being asked, and designed a research program to address them.

Why it was complex

The customer base was highly diverse, spanning 38 markets, multiple business models and very different levels of financial sophistication. Existing data was fragmented across teams. Getting to a unified, reliable picture of customer needs meant triangulating across qualitative fieldwork, large-scale quantitative studies and internal data, while keeping the output usable for audiences from product teams to executive leadership.

Research program and methods

  • In-depth interviews (customers)
  • In-depth interviews (account managers)
  • 38-market conjoint study
  • MaxDiff prioritisation
  • Customer panel
  • Experience measurement framework
  • Competitive analysis
  • Internal data triangulation

Approach highlights

The program was staged so that qualitative depth came before quantitative scale. Interviews with customers and account managers surfaced the mental models and pain points that informed the conjoint design, so we measured the right trade-offs. The 38-market conjoint then gave the statistical power to build credible business cases for specific product directions.

I piloted an experience measurement framework using a mix of existing survey data and newly designed measures. It produced a repeatable tracking tool that became the foundation for how the research team monitors customer health on an ongoing basis.

Throughout, I worked closely with Commercial and GTM teams, often the owners of customer relationships, so that research questions reflected their decision needs and findings translated into shared language across functions.

Impact

  • Research outputs directly shaped the company's 5-year B2B payments strategy, influencing product prioritisation, pricing frameworks and value propositions across the platform.
  • Co-created a Partner Financial Needs Framework, adopted organisation-wide as a shared decision-making artefact across Product, Strategy and Research.
  • Conjoint data provided the business case for a structural product change projected to unlock millions of new customer relationships.
  • The experience measurement framework was piloted and later adopted by the department as its standard ongoing tracking methodology.
  • Presented findings to senior leadership, including at a strategy planning offsite, where the insights drove prioritisation discussions directly.
  • Established a repeatable, multi-method research model for the B2B payments space that subsequent researchers could build on.
